# ABOUT ME

What is this about me? And what is this portfolio about? Well, the two elements that you can very clearly appreciate in both my life and this site are photography and music.

Chronologically, both things started at an early stage of my life: violin at 8 and photography at 13. The discrepancy, however, lies in the motivation. I asked to play an instrument, but photography was just an introductory course that my mother signed me up for without asking me. Still, the seed was planted.

Some years later, I got my first DSLR, just before starting my Bachelor's degree in viola in Lugano, and very soon I started connecting my interest in fellow musicians with the appealing abstraction of reality that happens when you have a camera in your hands.

Years passed, and I finished my formal studies in music in Lugano and Barcelona, while often having my camera with me: in orchestras, at conservatory parties, with friends, walking around the beautiful lakes of Lugano... Yet, I felt I needed a deeper understanding of visual language and of the journey of a photographer. So I decided to join Mestre Mateo in Santiago de Compostela for a Ciclo Superior de Fotografía.

After finishing art school, I kept following my journey, photographing the musicians and orchestras I was involved with, and little by little, I started to develop something you could call a style, while discovering what life is about.

With all of this background, some enthusiasm, some curiosity, and some craziness, I moved to Berlin in 2022, a European capital where classical music and photography (or visual arts in general) are both at their peak.

And here we are! Looking forward to continuing to discover myself, other people, and life in general, with the camera and with music.
