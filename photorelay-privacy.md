# PhotoRelay Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 5, 2026**

PhotoRelay ("PhotoRelay", "we", "us", or "our") is an Android application designed to help users transfer, organize, inspect, and manage photographs and their associated metadata.

This Privacy Policy explains how PhotoRelay handles information when you use the application.

## 1. Photos and Files

PhotoRelay is designed to process photographs locally on your device.

PhotoRelay does **not** upload your photographs, RAW files, JPEG files, EXIF metadata, filenames, or file paths to PhotoRelay servers.

PhotoRelay uses Android's Storage Access Framework to access files and folders that you explicitly select through the Android system file picker. The application does not request broad access to your device's storage.

Photo metadata, including information such as camera model, capture date, and star ratings, is processed locally on the device.

PhotoRelay may store a local database containing information associated with photographs that you have selected, such as filenames, folder paths, file sizes, modification dates, capture dates, camera models, and star ratings. This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to PhotoRelay.

## 2. Sharing Photographs

When you explicitly use PhotoRelay's sharing functionality, Android's standard system sharing interface is opened.

If you choose another application through that interface, such as a cloud-storage, messaging, or email application, the selected photographs are transferred to that application according to its own privacy policy and terms.

PhotoRelay does not control or receive information about what you subsequently do with photographs shared through another application.

## 3. Advertising

The free version of PhotoRelay may display advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**, a service operated by Google.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process certain information automatically for purposes including delivering advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud, and improving the advertising service. Depending on the circumstances and available device settings, this may include:

- IP address
- Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers
- Information about interactions with advertisements and the application
- Diagnostic and performance information
- Information required to provide and measure advertising services

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. The exact information processed may depend on the user's device, privacy settings, applicable regulations, and the AdMob features used by the application.

PhotoRelay does not use advertising services to upload or process the photographs or photo metadata stored on your device.

## 4. Advertising Choices and Consent

Where required by applicable law, PhotoRelay and its advertising providers will request the appropriate consent before using information for personalized advertising.

Users may also have advertising and privacy controls available through their Android device and Google settings.

The availability and operation of these controls may vary depending on the user's location, device, operating-system version, and applicable regulations.

## 5. Removing Advertisements

PhotoRelay may offer a one-time in-app purchase that removes advertisements from the application.

The purchase is processed through **Google Play Billing**. PhotoRelay does not receive or store your credit-card number, bank-account information, or other payment credentials.

Google Play processes the transaction and provides the application with information necessary to determine whether the purchase has been completed and whether the advertisement-removal entitlement should be enabled.

The purchase does not unlock additional application features. It only removes advertisements.

## 6. Information We Do Not Collect

PhotoRelay does not operate its own cloud storage or user-account system.

PhotoRelay does not collect or transmit:

- Your photographs
- RAW image files
- EXIF or other photo metadata
- Filenames or file paths
- Your contacts
- Your location
- Your camera or microphone recordings
- Your personal messages
- Your payment-card details

PhotoRelay also does not currently use its own analytics or crash-reporting service.

## 7. Local Processing and Storage

PhotoRelay performs its core photograph-processing functions directly on your Android device.

This includes scanning files, extracting metadata, reading ratings, filtering photographs, caching photograph information, copying files, and deleting files when explicitly requested by the user.

Locally stored application data may remain on the device until it is removed by the application, the user, or the Android operating system.

## 8. Third-Party Services

PhotoRelay may use the following third-party services:

### Google AdMob

Used to provide advertisements in the free version of PhotoRelay and to measure and support advertising delivery.

Google may process information as described in its own privacy policies and documentation.

### Google Play Billing

Used to process the one-time purchase that removes advertisements.

Payment processing is performed by Google Play.

These third-party services operate independently of PhotoRelay and may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

## 9. Data Security

PhotoRelay does not maintain a PhotoRelay server containing users' photographs or photo metadata.

Information transmitted by third-party services such as Google AdMob is handled using the security mechanisms provided by those services, including encryption in transit where applicable.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Children's Privacy

PhotoRelay is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through PhotoRelay.

## 11. Your Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights concerning personal information processed by third-party services used by the application, including rights of access, deletion, restriction, objection, or other rights provided by applicable law.

Because PhotoRelay itself does not maintain an account system or a server containing users' photographs, requests concerning data processed directly by PhotoRelay can be directed to us using the contact information below.

Requests concerning information processed by Google services may also need to be addressed to Google.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when new features or third-party services are added to PhotoRelay.

When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page.
